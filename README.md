## About Me

I'm **Christopher Honeywick Kalenge**, a Computer Science student at the **University of Dar es Salaam (UDSM), Tanzania**. My interests lie in software engineering, data science, machine learning, and building practical technology products.

I am particularly interested in how data can be transformed into reliable insights and how machine learning can solve real-world problems. I enjoy working across the development process, from designing applications and APIs to preparing datasets and exploring intelligent systems.

- 🎓 **Education:** BSc Computer Science — University of Dar es Salaam
- 💻 **Software Engineering:** Web applications, backend development, APIs, and databases

- 🤖 **Machine Learning:** Model-training fundamentals, computer vision, and data preparation
- 🧠 **Current interests:** Reliable ML pipelines, data-centric AI, and practical AI applications
- 🚀 **Building:** CiteLoom and other software projects

### Practical Computer Vision Experience

During my data-labeling work at Sky Connect, I used **Label Studio** to annotate thousands of images for a computer-vision workflow related to Serengeti tourism.

The annotation categories included tourist vehicles, other vehicles, humans, and non-human objects. This experience introduced me to an important part of the machine-learning lifecycle: preparing labeled image data that can be used to train and evaluate computer-vision models.

I'm interested in developing this experience further through machine learning, computer vision, data-centric AI, and better dataset quality practices.
